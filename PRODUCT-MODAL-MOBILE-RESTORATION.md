# Product modal and gallery restoration

Updated `index.html` while preserving the full customer-experience suite.

- Clicking anywhere on a product image now opens the product modal.
- Favourite controls still work independently and do not open the modal.
- Previous/next gallery arrows now have reliable positioning, stacking and mobile tap handling.
- Selecting a colour resets the gallery and immediately displays that colour's images.
- When a colour has no dedicated images, the product's main images are used as a safe fallback.
- The mobile modal now uses a full-height, independently scrollable layout.
- The `Commander` action remains sticky and visible at the bottom of the mobile modal.
- Existing AI assistant, WhatsApp, back-to-top, wishlist, cart, reviews, promotions, shipping and other features are preserved.
